## 0.2.1 - 2026-08-25
### Changed

- The catalog-not-registered guidance in `MessagingCatalog.RequireDescriptor`,
  `SubscriberRegistrar` and `AddD2SubscribersFromAssembly` says "the host's own catalog"
  where it said "the product catalog". The old wording used a term the reader had to
  translate; the exception now states what to register. Behavior is unchanged and the
  exception type, the constant it quotes and the rest of the text stay as they were.
## 0.2.0 - 2026-08-24
### Wire-breaking

- The `mq-messages` and `mq-subscriptions` catalogs this package generates from
  now have no entries: `MqMessages` and `MqSubscriptions` are empty, and
  `MqMessagesRegistry.ByConstant` / `MqSubscriptionsRegistry.ByConstant` are
  empty dictionaries. An application declares its message types and
  subscriptions in its own catalog files, which the generator compiles together
  with these into the application's own registry. The breaking-change gate allowed the break through
  its override.
### API-breaking

- `MqMessages.AuthKeyRotated` and `MqSubscriptions.KeyringRefresh` are gone with
  the entries that generated them.
### Added

- `MessagingCatalog` - the message and subscription descriptors one host
  publishes and consumes against, with `RequireDescriptor(Type)` performing the
  `[MqPub]` lookup and a per-instance resolution cache. `MessagingCatalog.Default`
  wraps the generated registries this package contains.
- `AddD2SubscribersFromAssembly(MessagingCatalog, params Assembly[])` - the
  assembly scan against a supplied catalog. The parameterless overload reads
  `MessagingCatalog.Default`.
